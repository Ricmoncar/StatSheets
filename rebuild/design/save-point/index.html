Why this direction

  • Instant readBlack, white, orange commands, yellow over red bars. Nobody has to be told what game this is.
  • One systemSix pieces build every page: box, command, menu list, dialogue, bar, sprite. Legacy pages map onto them.
  • Real pixelsNo glow, no blur, no smooth gradient. Dither, banded ramps and stepped sprite animation only.
  • Motion is earnedCommon things sit still. Save stars, rare traits and rolls move, at 6 to 12 frames a second.

* DESIGN NOTES

Enough to build it. Everything below is live: change the accent and the ramps, frames and sprites redraw.

Pixel grid

One art pixel, --px: 3 CSS px on desktop, 2 below 640 px wide. Every border, gap, sprite and texture is a whole number of art pixels: write sizes as calc(N * var(--px)) or the --u1 to --u16 tokens. Never use percentages for anything drawn.

  • Panel frame 2 art px. Controls and cards 1 art px. Rarity frames 4 art px with corner ornaments.
  • Shadows are hard offsets of 1 or 2 art px. No blur, no glow, no smooth gradient, no radius.
  • Fades are 4x4 Bayer dither or banded ramps with hard stops at whole art pixels.
  • image-rendering: pixelated is set on the root and inherited by every image, mask and canvas.

Palette tokens

Accent recolouring

The character colour is the base, --a2. JS converts it to HSL and builds 5 steps: shadows shift hue toward blue-violet (250°) and darken, lights shift toward yellow (60°) and brighten, the classic pixel art ramp. --a-text is the first step with 4.5:1 contrast on black, so dark colours like deep violet stay legible. Orange hovers toward yellow, exactly like the battle menu.

One-colour sprites are CSS masks filled with a token, so they recolour for free. Multi-colour sprites (portraits, coins, frames, backgrounds) keep palette-index grids and redraw to canvas when the accent changes, then publish as data URL custom properties.

Frame system

Frames are 9-slice sprites drawn as border-image: corners keep their art, edges are 1 art px tiles that repeat, the centre fills. Any size works and no pixel stretches. Plain panels use a 2 art px solid border with the same result. A panel title sits on the top edge on black, prefixed with "*".

Animation rules

KindRateHow
Sprite loops6 to 8 fpsStrip sheets, steps(frames) on background-position
Backgrounds6 to 12 fpsCanvas at art res, one shared frame timer, off-screen ones skip
Texture scroll1 art px a framesteps(tile) over a seamless tile
UI moves2 to 4 framesSOUL hops, toasts, drawer: stepped, snapped to art px
Typewriter30 chars/sBattle text speed; instant on reduced motion

No eased tweens on pixel art, ever. Motion is earned: common UI is still, rarity and rolls move.

Performance mode and reduced motion

  • PERF on: backgrounds draw one frame and stop, sprite loops pause on frame 0, the Hexxed glitch stops. Dither, frames and sprites stay, they cost nothing.
  • Every animated sprite is designed so frame 0 reads on its own: the Mythic frame still shows all seven colours, Hexxed still shows static.
  • prefers-reduced-motion does the same plus: typewriter text appears whole, the SOUL jumps instead of hopping, toasts appear in place.
  • The frame timer runs at most one rAF loop, draws only canvases on screen, and stops while the tab is hidden.

Building blocks

  • Box: the battle box. Every panel, dialog and page section.
  • Command: icon plus caps label. SOUL replaces the icon on hover and focus.
  • Menu list: "*" items with a SOUL on the current one. Tabs, sidebar, context menu, select, dialogue choices.
  • Dialogue: portrait and typewriter text. Modals, confirms, empty states, toasts.
  • Bar: banded yellow over red, 20 segments, magenta for prestige tiers like KR.
  • Sprite: palette-index grid, mask or canvas, stepped animation.

Secondary pages

  • Utilities: a grid of boxes, one tool each, commands along the floor of every box.
  • Gallery: portrait frames in a grid; opening one shows it in a dialogue box with its caption typed out.
  • Encyclopaedia: menu list on the left, reading pane box on the right, entries open with "*" headings.
  • Playlists: menu list of tracks, the SOUL marks the playing one, the player bar is shared with the sheet.
  • BK's Corner: posts are dialogue boxes with BK's portrait.
  • Spin a Baddie: the wheel is a canvas sprite in a battle box, the SOUL is the pointer, results reveal like a trait roll.
  • Admin: tables in boxes, danger commands only, every destructive action goes through the confirm dialogue.

All art on this page is drawn by code at load: sprite grids, canvas and CSS. No images are fetched. Character names other than BLACKJACK and Cappy are placeholders.
