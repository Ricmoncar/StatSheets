* AT 390 WIDE

The same markup at phone width. The art pixel drops from 3 to 2 CSS px, the sidebar becomes a drawer that steps in from the left (focus moves to search, the sheet goes inert), the seven page commands fold into MENU, tabs stay on one scrolling row, and every hit area grows to 16 art px.

* Every page is a battle box now. White frame, black void, the SOUL shows where you are.

* Behind the boxes, the Underground: Ruins brick, Waterfall, Snowdin. All drawn on one grid, all recoloured by the character.

Why this direction

  • Instant readBlack, white, orange commands, yellow over red bars. Nobody has to be told what game this is.
  • One systemSix pieces build every page: box, command, menu list, dialogue, bar, sprite. Legacy pages map onto them.
  • Real placesBoxes hang from Ruins brick lintels, the sheet stands on a tiled floor with leaves falling, and every trait has its own animated art window.
  • Real pixelsNo glow, no blur, no smooth gradient. Dither, banded ramps and stepped sprite animation only.
  • Motion is earnedCommon things sit still. Save stars, rare traits and rolls move, at 6 to 12 frames a second.

* DESIGN NOTES

Enough to build it. Everything below is live: change the accent and the ramps, tiles, frames and sprites redraw.

Pixel grid

One art pixel, --px: 3 CSS px on desktop, 2 below 640 px wide. Every border, gap, sprite and texture is a whole number of art pixels: write sizes as calc(N * var(--px)) or the --u1 to --u16 tokens. Never use percentages for anything drawn.

  • Panel frame 2 art px. Controls and cards 1 art px. Rarity frames 4 art px with corner ornaments.
  • Shadows are hard offsets of 1 or 2 art px. No blur, no glow, no smooth gradient, no radius.
  • Fades are 4x4 Bayer dither or banded ramps with hard stops at whole art pixels.
  • image-rendering: pixelated is set on the root and inherited by every image, mask and canvas. Background canvases are sized in whole art pixels, so their scale is exactly 3 (or 2).
  • Grid snap: after layout, one pass nudges every sprite, canvas and frame by its sub-pixel remainder (translate), so fr columns and centring never put an art pixel across two device pixels. In Angular this is one directive on the shell.
  • Type: data, names, dialogue and commands sit exactly on the grid. 16 px labels and body copy use a 2 px glyph pixel on desktop: the one allowed mixel, never inside art.

Palette tokens

31 system colours and 8 material colours, plus 9 derived from the accent (below). Each rarity frame adds 2 tints.

Accent recolouring

The character colour is the base, --a2. JS converts it to HSL and builds 5 steps: shadows shift hue toward blue-violet (250°) and darken, lights shift toward yellow (60°) and brighten, the classic pixel art ramp. --m0 to --m3 are the same hue at about a third of the chroma and very dark: the stone that boxes hang from and the floor the sheet stands on, so the accent runs through the whole page without turning it one colour.

--a-text is the first step with 4.5:1 on black. Text sitting on a step uses that step's ink (--a-ink, --a3-ink, --a1-ink): black or white, whichever has more contrast, so deep violet buttons get white labels and lemon ones get black.

One-colour sprites are CSS masks filled with a token, so they recolour for free. Multi-colour sprites and tiles keep palette-index grids and redraw to canvas when the accent changes, then publish as data URL custom properties.

Proof: the same portrait, lintel, flagstone and leaf from six ramps, with a0 to a4 and m0 to m3 under each.

Frame system

Rarity frames are 9-slice sprites drawn as border-image: corners keep their art, edges are 1 art px tiles that repeat, the centre fills. Any size works and no pixel stretches. The battle box is a plain 2 art px white border. A box with a title hangs from a LINTEL: a 12 art px band tiled with repeat-x under the top edge, the title on a black plate. The sheet header stands in front of a 24 art px WALL. Tiles only ever repeat, so any width works.

No eased tweens on pixel art, ever. Motion is earned: common UI is still, rarity, places and rolls move.

Performance mode and reduced motion

  • PERF on: backgrounds draw one frame and stop, sprite loops pause on frame 0, the Hexxed glitch stops. Dither, tiles, frames and sprites stay; they cost nothing once drawn.
  • Every animated sprite is designed so frame 0 reads on its own: the Mythic rim still shows all seven colours, the Hexxed eye is still torn.
  • Reduced motion and MOTION off: loops pause on frame 0, but entrances (toasts, the roll shake) are switched off with animation:none, not paused, so nothing is parked off-screen. Typewriter text appears whole, the SOUL jumps instead of hopping.
  • One rAF loop runs every canvas, draws only what IntersectionObserver says is on screen, and stops while the tab is hidden. The toolbar shows its measured cost per tick. Panels with ticking text use contain:content.

Accessibility baseline

  • Focus is a 1 art px #FFFF00 ring with a 1 art px gap, on everything.
  • Drawer toggle: aria-expanded and aria-controls; opening moves focus to search; Esc and the scrim close it and return focus; the sheet behind is inert.
  • MENU command: aria-haspopup="menu", arrow keys move between pages, Esc closes. Toggles are role="switch", toasts are a role="status" live region.
  • Touch: at the phone layout every hit area is at least 16 art px (32 CSS px). Tabs stay on one row and scroll, with a dithered fade showing there is more.
  • Text on any accent step uses that step's ink token, never a fixed white.

Building blocks

  • Box: the battle box, with a lintel when it has a title. Every panel, dialog and page section.
  • Command: icon plus caps label. The SOUL replaces the icon on hover and focus and the command turns #FFFF00.
  • Menu list: "*" items with a SOUL on the current one. Nav, tabs, sidebar, context menu, select, dialogue choices.
  • Dialogue: portrait and typewriter text. Modals, confirms, empty states, toasts.
  • Bar: banded yellow over red, 20 segments, magenta for prestige tiers like KR. The stat's own colour is on its icon and label.
  • Sprite and Tile: palette-index grids drawn once, published as CSS variables. In Angular, one AtlasService (icon(), sprite(), frame(), tile()) owns them and redraws on an accent change; components only use classes and tokens.

Secondary pages

  • Navigation: all seven pages as commands from 1720 app px; below that they fold into one MENU command with the same list, so no page disappears.
  • Utilities: a grid of boxes, one tool each, commands along the floor of every box.
  • Gallery: portrait frames in a grid; opening one shows it in a dialogue box with its caption typed out.
  • Encyclopaedia: menu list on the left, reading pane box on the right, entries open with "*" headings.
  • Playlists: menu list of tracks, the SOUL marks the playing one, the player bar is shared with the sheet.
  • BK's Corner: posts are dialogue boxes with BK's portrait, on the Ruins floor.
  • Spin a Baddie: the wheel is a canvas sprite in a battle box, the SOUL is the pointer, results reveal like a trait roll.
  • Admin: tables in boxes, danger commands only, every destructive action goes through the confirm dialogue.
